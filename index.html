Selected Projects

  • Kriti - A Lore Architect and Writing Workspace Kriti is a single-screen creative workspace for serious fiction writers. Unlike tools that split your manuscript (Scrivener) from your world notes (Obsidian/World Anvil) from your AI assistant (Novelcrafter sidebar), Kriti fuses them into a triptych interface: the manuscript lives in the center, the Codex (auto-detected continuity & entities) lives beside it, and the World Catalog lives below it. There are no broken tabs, no broken context.
